Addons are optional add-ons that change how the editor looks and behaves, from small conveniences to whole new tools. Each one can be turned on or off independently, and many have their own settings. They affect only your editor; they do not change how a project runs when shared.

## Opening addon settings

Open the **Edit** menu and choose **Addons**. This opens the addon settings window, a searchable list of every addon with a toggle for each.

## Using the list

- **Enable or disable** an addon with its checkbox. Changes take effect immediately.
- **Per-addon settings**: expand an addon to see its own options (checkboxes, dropdowns, colour pickers, and so on).
- **Presets**: some addons offer named presets that apply a bundle of settings at once.
- **Reset**: each addon has a reset button that restores its defaults.
- **Search** by name, description, or setting, and **filter by tags** to narrow the list. Addons are tagged with markers such as **recommended**, **theme**, **beta**, **new**, and **danger** (potentially disruptive).

At the bottom of the window, **Reset all** restores every addon to its defaults, and **Export** and **Import** save and load your whole addon configuration as a file, which is handy for moving your setup between browsers or sharing it.

## Notes

- Some features that began as addons are now built into RemixWarp directly and are configured on the [settings](/editor/settings) pages instead of here.
- Addon settings are stored in your browser and sync to your account when you are signed in.
